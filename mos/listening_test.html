Listening test · श्रवण परीक्षण

हिंदी वाक् श्रवण परीक्षण

Rate short Hindi speech clips for distortion and listening effort (ITU-T P.800). About 12 minutes.

Before you start · शुरू करने से पहले

You will hear 42 short Hindi sentences, one at a time. Some are natural recordings and some are generated by a machine. Listen to each clip to the end, then answer two questions about it. You can replay a clip as often as you like.

आप एक-एक करके 42 छोटे हिंदी वाक्य सुनेंगे। कुछ असली रिकॉर्डिंग हैं, कुछ मशीन से बनाए गए हैं। हर क्लिप को पूरा सुनें, फिर उसके बारे में दो सवालों के जवाब दें। आप क्लिप को जितनी बार चाहें दोबारा सुन सकते हैं।

Rate each clip on its own; there are no right answers. Your progress is saved, so you can stop and come back.

Three example clips · तीन उदाहरण

These show the range of quality you will hear, from best to worst. They are not rated. Listen to all three, then start.
